Screen 1: Welcome Splash & Hook

Mascot Role: Buddy the Puzzle Fox peeks from the side (half-hidden, curious expression) — tail curled into a question mark.
Illustrations: Background subtle puzzle pieces floating and connecting (soft glow on connect).
Interaction: Tap the fox → it waves and says (speech bubble): "Hi! I'm Buddy. Ready to make friends the fun way? No awkward intros—just do cool stuff together!"
Iconography: Small icons next to myth cards (broken heart for "swiping fails", puzzle for "tasks win").
Typography:
Headline: Bold rounded sans-serif (e.g., Poppins Bold or custom rounded variant) — large, warm orange.
Body: Clean readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Nunito) — medium weight, dark gray/teal.
Animations: Text fades in sequentially; fox "pops" in with bounce.

Engagement Boost: Fox reacts to card flips (thumbs up on correct myth).

Screen 2: Value Prop Deep Dive (Carousel)

Mascot Role: Buddy appears in each card illustration — e.g., wearing a mask in anonymity card, holding puzzle pieces in tasks card, climbing a ladder in trust card.
Illustrations: Hand-drawn style, soft shadows, pastel gradients; each card has a small animated fox action (e.g., high-five on tap).
Interaction: Swipe cards; tap fox in corner → short animation (e.g., fox solves mini-puzzle) + encouraging bubble: "See? We build trust step by step!"
Iconography: Simple line icons with teal fill (mask for anonymity, handshake for tasks, ladder steps for trust).
Typography: Card headlines in playful bold (e.g., Fredoka One), subtext in lighter weight.
Theme Tie-in: Carousel ends with fox saying: "Your turn—tell me what you love!"

Screen 3: Interests Selection

Mascot Role: Buddy sits at bottom, holding a big empty puzzle board. As user taps interests, pieces fly from board to fox's paws (animation).
Illustrations: Interest chips as colorful puzzle pieces with icons (music note, dumbbell, gamepad, book, chef hat, controller).
Interaction: Tap chip → fox cheers (small jump + confetti); if over limit, fox gently shakes head with "Too many puzzles! Pick your favorites."
Iconography: Rounded, filled icons matching palette (orange border on selected).
Typography: Chip text small + bold; headline "What Sparks Your Curiosity?" (fox-themed wording).

Screen 4: Energy Level Picker

Mascot Role: Buddy mirrors each energy: Low (yawning fox on couch), Medium (balanced fox walking), High (fox with rocket tail).
Illustrations: Three illustrated scenes with fox in different poses.
Interaction: Tap button → fox enlarges + animates matching pose + sound (soft chime); selected one gets glowing outline.
Iconography: Energy icons (snail, turtle, rocket) integrated with fox.
Typography: Buttons use large, friendly text; subtext explains task fit.

Screen 5: Availability Selector

Mascot Role: Buddy holds a calendar; taps highlight time slots with fox "marking" them (checkmark tail swipe).
Illustrations: Time icons stylized as puzzle timers (clock with pieces).
Interaction: Tap → fox nods excitedly; final CTA makes fox jump with "Let's find puzzle partners!"
Iconography: Clock, calendar, weekend sun—teal/orange.
Typography: Consistent with previous; CTA in bold orange.

Screen 6: Matching Tease & Completion

Mascot Role: Buddy "searches" (magnifying glass over eyes), then cheers when match found: "Yay! Puzzle buddy incoming!"
Illustrations: Loading = fox assembling puzzle of two silhouettes connecting.
Interaction: Progress bar fills with puzzle pieces; fox high-fives user (tap screen for extra animation).
Theme Tie-in: Ends with: "Welcome to TaskFriends, [Nickname]! Let's build some awesome memories with Buddy guiding the way."

Overall App-Wide Theme Guidelines (Apply Beyond Onboarding)

Color Palette: Orange (energy/fun), Teal (trust/calm), Soft gray/white backgrounds, Purple accents (unlocks/progress).
Illustrations Style: 2D flat-kawaii with soft gradients, rounded edges, subtle textures (fur, puzzle grain). Use for empty states ("Buddy's waiting for a match…"), success modals, task intros.
Interactions & Animations: Micro-animations via Expo Lottie or RN Reanimated — fox waves on load, puzzle connects on task complete, ears perk on unlocks.
Iconography: Custom set — puzzle-based (e.g., puzzle piece for tasks, locked/unlocked padlock with fox tail, ladder steps).
Typography Stack:
Headlines/CTA: Rounded bold sans (Fredoka One or Poppins ExtraBold) — playful, friendly.
Body/Microcopy: Clean sans (Inter or Nunito) — readable, calm.
Fox speech bubbles: Handwritten-style font (e.g., Comic Neue or custom) for personality.

Consistency Rule: Buddy appears in ~70% of key moments (not overwhelming); always positive/expressive to reinforce safety and fun.
