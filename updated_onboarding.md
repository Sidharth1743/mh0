Mascot Adjustment

New Mascot: Subtle, abstract “Trust Companion” — no longer a cartoon animal. Instead: a minimalist, elegant abstract geometric fox silhouette (or simply a stylized abstract shape: soft rounded lines forming a gentle, hooded/curved figure symbolizing curiosity + protection).
Style: Line art / duotone / very low detail (think modern minimalist icons like those in Apple Fitness+ or Calm illustrations).
Evolution: Starts as a faint outline (anonymous/trust-building phase), gradually fills with soft gradient as trust unlocks.
Usage: Appears sparingly — corner accents, loading states, success animations — never dominant or “talking” with speech bubbles. No big eyes, no waving. Feels sophisticated, not playful-cute.


Color Palette (Calm, Trust, Safety – Adult Social Apps)

Primary: Deep indigo / soft navy (#2C3E50 – #34495E) — trust, depth, calm (like Discord/Bumble dark accents).
Secondary/Accent: Warm muted teal (#26A69A or softer #4DB6AC) — safety, growth, subtle energy (Headspace-like).
Neutral Background: Off-white / light cool gray (#F8FAFC / #F1F5F9) — clean, airy, reduces eye strain.
Progress/Unlocks: Subtle gradient purple-teal (#7C3AED → #4DB6AC) — premium feel without brightness.
Buttons/CTA: Soft teal or indigo fill with white text; subtle hover/shadow on press (iOS haptic + micro-animation).
Avoid: Bright orange, rainbow, primary reds/yellows — too energetic/childish.

Illustration Style

Modern minimalist line art + soft gradients (2025 trend).
Abstract, symbolic: Puzzle pieces as clean geometric shapes (not colorful blocks), connecting lines fading in, soft glows on connection.
No cartoon characters dominating; use subtle abstract motifs (curved lines, overlapping forms symbolizing gradual reveal).
High-quality, scalable vectors — feels premium like Calm onboarding or Apple’s wellness flows.

Iconography

Clean, outlined icons (SF Symbols base + custom tweaks).
Stroke width consistent (2–2.5 pt), rounded corners.
Colors: Monochrome gray for inactive (#64748B), accent teal/indigo for active/selected.
Examples: Interests → simple line icons (headphones, dumbbell, code brackets, book, pot, gamepad) with no fill until selected.

Typography Stack (Professional iOS 2025 Clean Sans-Serif)

Headlines / Titles: SF Pro Display Bold / Semibold (Apple’s native — or fallback: Inter ExtraBold / Figtree Bold) — large (32–48 pt), letter spacing tight for premium feel.
Subheadings / Body: SF Pro Text Regular / Medium (or Inter Regular / Figtree Regular) — 16–20 pt body, excellent readability, high x-height.
Microcopy / Buttons: SF Pro Text Medium — crisp at small sizes.
Overall: Variable fonts if possible (better performance), high contrast, generous line height (1.4–1.6), no playful/rounded fonts like Fredoka or Poppins for body.
